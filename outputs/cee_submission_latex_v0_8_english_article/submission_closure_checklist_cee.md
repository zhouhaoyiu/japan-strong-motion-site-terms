## Current scientific boundary

The manuscript supports a record-to-response-spectrum sensitivity claim. It shows that 1--3 s station residuals in Japanese strong-motion records are repeatable, predictable from public site variables, and large enough to shift official J-SHIS long-period response-spectrum ordinates. It does not claim a validated official J-SHIS production PSHA rerun. Public source-parameter checks document the input-chain coverage and sampled-grid consistency tests, while source recurrence, spatial correlation, model uncertainty, and logic-tree aggregation remain in the official J-SHIS product.
